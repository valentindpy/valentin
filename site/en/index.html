How it works

Four steps, and you take only one

  1. You describe

    The form takes less than a minute. One photo replaces ten questions, and is often enough to quote without a visit.

  2. We choose

    The firm that fits the trade, the town and the deadline — from those whose insurance file is up to date with us.

  3. We compare

    Several firms quote against a single specification. The quotes compare line by line, and we negotiate before passing them on to you.

  4. We follow through

    Through to sign-off, with photographs of the finished work. One point of contact from start to finish.

What never changes

The quote and the invoice are in your name.

We do not carry out the work and we sell nothing. The firm issues its quote directly to you, the choice remains yours, and you pay us nothing. We are paid by the firm, on work actually carried out.

About us

VD Services

VD Services was founded in Mandelieu-la-Napoule by Valentin Dupuy, to solve a problem everybody knows: finding the right firm, getting a quote, and making sure the work is done properly.

Our trade is the network and the method. Every partner firm keeps a current file with us: ten-year structural insurance, public liability, URSSAF compliance certificate. When a request comes in, we pick the firm that fits the trade, the town and the deadline.

We have everyone quote against a single specification so the prices can be compared. And we stay reachable until the job is finished.

I started VD Services because I spent years watching people lose weeks over a quote, and good tradespeople lose days on enquiries that led nowhere. Somebody was missing in between.

Valentin DupuyFounder, Mandelieu-la-Napoule

For trades

Become a partner

You are a tradesperson or a building firm in the area, and you are looking for qualified work — not a race to the lowest bid.

What you get

  • Qualified enquiries in your trade, in your town, with the address, the nature of the job and photographs.
  • A single contact who has already spoken to the client and checked the enquiry is genuine.
  • A clear specification to price against, instead of a vague phone call and a wasted trip.
  • No joining fee, no subscription, no exclusivity imposed.

What we ask of you

  • Current insurance: ten-year structural cover, public liability, URSSAF compliance certificate.
  • A reply within forty-eight hours, even if it is a no.
  • Appointments kept, and the client told if you are running late.
  • A site left clean, and a photograph of the finished work.

How we are paid

An introducer’s commission, paid by you, only on work actually carried out and paid for. Nothing to pay to join the network, nothing up front, and nothing at all if the job does not happen.

The rate is agreed together when we sign the agreement, based on your trade, your margins and the volume we bring you. We discuss it at the first meeting, before you sign anything.
